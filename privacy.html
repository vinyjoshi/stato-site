What Stato is

Stato is a mobile app that shows greeting images and short videos and puts your own name and photo (or a short video of yourself) on them, so you can share them with family and friends.

What stays on your phone

  • Your name and profile photo. You enter them in the app. They are stored only on your phone and are never uploaded to us.
  • Videos you record or choose. The app reads the clip on your phone to put it inside the greeting video, and copies its sound. The clip and the finished video are not uploaded to us. Temporary copies are kept in the app's cache until Android clears it or you delete your data.
  • Your reminder time and the count of free greetings you made today.

What the app sends or receives

  • Greeting feed. The app downloads a public file of greeting texts and colours from our hosting provider. Like any web request, the provider may log your IP address and the time of the request. We do not attach your name, photo or any account to this request.
  • Purchases. If you subscribe, payment is handled by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase information from Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.
  • Sharing. When you share or save a greeting, it goes where you choose (for example WhatsApp or your gallery). Those images and videos contain the name and photo you put on them. What happens after you share is up to you and the app you share to.

Permissions

  • Photos, camera and microphone. The app uses Android's own picker and camera screens. You choose each photo or video; the app cannot browse your gallery.
  • Notifications. Optional. Used only for the daily reminder you switch on.
  • Internet. To download the feed and check your subscription.

What we do not do

Stato has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs at the time of writing. We do not sell your data. If this ever changes, we will update this page and the app before it takes effect.

Deleting your data

Open your profile menu in the app and tap "मेरा डेटा हटाएँ" (Delete my data). This removes your profiles, photos, reminder and the downloaded feed from your phone. Uninstalling the app also removes everything stored by the app. Purchase records are held by Google Play and RevenueCat under their own policies; you can ask us to request their deletion.

Your rights

Because your name, photo and videos stay on your phone, you already control them. If you want to ask about, correct or delete anything we may hold about you, or to raise a complaint, write to viyj2000@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

Children

Stato is meant for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
